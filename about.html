About Despatch
Built for the businesses that can't afford to miss a contract.

Despatch is a daily GCC government tender intelligence service. Every morning, we deliver a curated digest of active procurement opportunities across Bahrain, Oman, Kuwait, UAE, and Saudi Arabia — directly to your inbox.

Government procurement in the GCC is worth hundreds of billions of dollars annually. Yet most of that opportunity goes uncaptured — not because companies lose bids, but because they never knew the tender existed. Portals are scattered, inconsistently updated, and time-consuming to monitor manually.

Despatch solves this. We monitor every major GCC procurement portal daily, filter and classify tenders by sector and urgency, and deliver a clean, readable digest before your workday starts. No logins. No portal-checking. Just your morning brief.

What we monitor

Bahrain Tender Board, Oman eTendering Portal, Kuwait CAPT, Abu Dhabi Government Procurement Gateway, UAE Federal Ministry of Finance, and Saudi Arabia's Etimad platform. Qatar coverage is coming soon.

How it works

Every morning, our system pulls the latest tenders from each portal, removes expired listings, classifies them by sector, and flags new listings and urgent deadlines. The result is a structured digest — new tenders first, critical deadlines highlighted in red, urgent in amber — delivered to your inbox before 7am Bahrain time.

Who it's for

Procurement managers, business development teams, contractors, consultancies, and suppliers who actively bid on GCC government contracts. If you currently spend time manually checking procurement portals, Despatch eliminates that entirely.
